Glossary
Schedule
A time window during which a doctor is available. Container for slots (e.g. "Apr 1–30, 08:00–18:00").
Slot
A specific bookable unit within a schedule (e.g. "09:00–09:30"). Patients book slots, not schedules.
Free
Open and available for patient booking.
Busy
Already booked by a patient.
Unavailable
Blocked by the doctor — break or admin time. Cannot be booked.
