\chapter*{Abstract}
% \addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\label{chap:abstract}
Healthcare today is characterized by fragmented experiences where patient medical information is often dispersed across various institutions and providers. This project proposes a comprehensive, patient-centric healthcare application designed to consolidate all medical reports into a single, secure, and unified platform. The system employs Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure privacy and controlled information sharing. To enhance clinical decision-making, the platform features a medical history visualization graph for tracking health trends and integrates a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-enabled AI model to generate diagnostic reports and insights. By transforming isolated data into an interconnected ecosystem, this project aims to establish meaningful relationships between patients, caregivers, and doctors for personalized healthcare delivery.
