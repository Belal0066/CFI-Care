Glossary
Schedule
A time window during which a doctor is available. It acts as a container for slots (e.g. "April 1–30, 08:00–18:00").
Slot
A specific bookable time unit within a schedule (e.g. "09:00–09:30"). Patients book slots, not schedules.
Free
Slot is open and available for patient booking.
Busy
Slot is already booked by a patient.
Unavailable
Slot is blocked by the doctor (e.g. break, admin time). Cannot be booked.
